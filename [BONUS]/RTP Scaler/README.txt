=============
= RTP Scaler ==================================================================================================
=============
Thank you for supporting SRPG Engine MV.
This tools are made as an expression of my gratitude for your support to the SRPG Engine MV project.

RTP Scaler is a collection of automatic batch script that can help you scaling RPG Maker assets up to 4 times.
It is designed for pixelart and I plan to upgrade it in the future to support general style picture as well.

Use cases:
----------
- Use RPG Maker XP, VX, and VX Ace assets in RPG Maker MV or MV;
- You can save your money by making small assets which cost less and enlarging them with this tool;
- You can use it if you want to support HD resolution for your game.

How to Use:
-----------
- Backup your assets first;
- Copy your picture assets in the same directory as this scaler;
- Drag your PNG to [Scale 1.5x].bat, [Scale 2x].bat, [Scale 3x].bat, or [Scale 4x].bat;
- Wait for the process;
- There will be 2 files. The rich color one and the one that retain its original pixelart number of colors.
